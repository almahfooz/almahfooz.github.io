Background

Research & practice.

My doctoral research at the International Islamic University Malaysia developed an open science model for research data management at Malaysia’s National Institutes of Health. I continue to explore what makes data sharing useful, practical, and responsible in institutional settings.

My academic experience includes a postdoctoral research fellowship at the International Islamic University Malaysia (2025–2026) and a research fellowship at Gulf University, Bahrain (2025–2026). Across teaching, research, and postgraduate mentoring, I work with both quantitative and qualitative approaches.

In consultations, I help researchers connect a question to the evidence it needs. In editing, I work with authors to make that evidence and its meaning easier for readers to follow.
